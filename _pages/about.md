Welcome! I am a Biostatistician working at the interface of spatial omics, host-microbiome interactions, and cancer biology.
I am currently a Research Fellow working with [Prof. Evan T. Keller](https://medschool.umich.edu/profile/1464/evan-t-keller) in the Division of Urologic Oncology, Department of Urology, University of Michigan. 

Before joining the Department of Urology, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Biostatistics at the University of Michigan, where I worked with [Prof. Xiang Zhou](https://xiangzhou.github.io/). I received my Ph.D. in Biostatistics from Pennsylvania State University under the supervision of [Prof. Xiang Zhan](https://sites.google.com/view/zhangroup/home). My dissertation focused on adaptive statistical methods for microbiome association analysis. I also hold a M. Stat. (Master of Statistics) degree from the [Indian Statistical Institute](https://www.isical.ac.in/index), and a B.Sc. degree with Honours in Statistics from [Asutosh College](https://asutoshcollege.in/new-web/), University of Calcutta.

My methodological work focuses on statistical genomics, kernel methods, feature selection, multi-modal data integration, and deep learning. I am particularly interested in developing data-adaptive computational methods that can handle the defining challenges of modern biomedical data: high dimensionality, sparsity, complex dependence structures, spatial organization, and heterogeneous signal patterns across samples, cells, and tissue regions.
